description=PROJECT M.O.R.T.I.S.
description=More Original Reanimated Threats Injection System
description=
description=Project M.O.R.T.I.S. is a public infected and living-entity framework. It provides reusable identity, behavior, anatomy, trauma, movement, grouping, hunger, persistence, and diagnostics APIs for extension mods.
description=
description=Important: Framework, Not a Zombie Pack
description=
description=Project M.O.R.T.I.S. does not add custom zombie types by itself. It wraps vanilla entities and supplies the systems other mods use. For example, Project Nightfall owns its Transforming, Feral, and Smart Feral zombies and registers them through this API.
description=
description=Current Features
description=
description=- Types, traits, behaviors, components, species, and event dispatch
description=
description=- Persistent identity, assignment authority, replication, and reconciliation
description=
description=- Locomotion, durability, appearance, collision, and balance profiles
description=

description=

description=- Authoritative anatomical attachment install/remove state, persistence, replay protection, and neutral events
description=
