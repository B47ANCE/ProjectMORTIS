## Remaining gaps and release blockers

- **Native locomotion / authority:** MORTIS canonical authority is server-owned;
  native simulation ownership, owner transfer, observer synchronization, actual
  motion and headless events remain NOT TESTED. Five matching fields do not
  reconstruct unobserved animation/lunge/helper bookkeeping. No new production
  Active Hours controller is safe to enable from this audit alone.
- **Lifecycle:** originals now have a journal, but consumer leases, priorities,
  ceilings and session/policy are not replayed after restart/wrapper loss. Early
  unloaded components and corrupt/newer records refuse mutation. Recovery needs
  coordinated explicit clear and actual save/rejoin/restart validation; no blind
  reset, legacy-original guessing or automatic owner takeover is implemented.
- **Consumer contracts:** Nightfall's consume-only effigy provider still needs
  its own transaction migration. This MORTIS-only iteration changes no consumer
  repository. Consumers must also handle blocked profile/assignment changes
  while their movement leases remain active. Never discard another owner merely
  to obtain a successful return.
- **Roadmap implementation:** solar sampling, Night/Day/Both policy, authoritative
  localized sandbox options, durable per-life/config-revision choices, bounded
  population lifecycle and standalone parity remain incomplete. The existing
  weighted CDF experiment is offline mathematics, not installed gameplay.
- **Evidence/localization:** the complete English names/tooltips and JSON checks
  do not prove non-English fluency, native fallback, Linux execution or actual
  game UI. Anatomy, wounds, corpse/material conservation, hunger, logical groups,
  archive/history and replication have host regressions; their documented native
  SP/dedicated gates remain open. Passing tests are bounded evidence, not mod
  completeness.
